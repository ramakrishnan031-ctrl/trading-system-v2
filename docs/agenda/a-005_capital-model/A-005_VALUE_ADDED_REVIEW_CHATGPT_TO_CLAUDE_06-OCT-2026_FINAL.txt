1. Q-22 — V2 CAPITAL / QUANTITY RULE
------------------------------------
Rama has now clarified the intended V2 flow.

Interpret the intended V2 sequence as:

1) TOTAL OPENING CASH CAPITAL
   ↓
2) SPLIT INTO INTRADAY / DELIVERY SEGMENT CASH
   according to config.yaml
   ↓
3) DETERMINE THE SEGMENT'S MAXIMUM FILLED-TRADE LIMIT
   from config.yaml
   ↓
4) BASE MAXIMUM CASH CAPITAL PER TRADE
      = locked segment cash capital
        / configured maximum filled-trade limit
   ↓
5) APPLY THE RELEVANT STRATEGY YAML CAPITAL CAP
   The effective cash-cap for the trade is the LOWER of:
      - global/config-derived maximum cash per trade
      - strategy.yaml maximum cash allocation/cap
      - current available segment cash
   The units MUST be the same before MIN() is applied.
   Do not compare cash with notional without explicitly converting units.
   ↓
6) APPLY BROKER MARGIN
      permitted notional
        = effective cash allocation / required margin %
   Example only; values are not frozen.
   If broker margin is 20%, ₹X cash permits ₹X/0.20 notional.
   If broker margin is 50%, ₹X cash permits ₹X/0.50 notional.
   If broker margin data is unavailable under the already-discussed V2 rule,
   use 100% cash / no invented leverage, with alert.
   ↓
7) CALCULATE ORDER QUANTITY USING STRATEGY RISK / SL
   Risk-per-trade percentage comes from the relevant strategy.yaml.
   Risk/SL-based quantity is the primary quantity calculation.
   The capital-derived permitted notional is a CEILING on that quantity.

   Conceptually:
      risk_qty = risk budget / expected SL distance
      capital_qty = floor(permitted notional / intended entry price)
      final_qty = lower permitted quantity, after approved instrument/
                  lot/tick/other guards.

   Do not revert to the old spreadsheet-only

3. DYNAMIC / LATER-TRADE CAPITAL
-------------------------------
The base per-trade amount is the configured maximum allocation, but the actual
allocation for a later trade is:

    actual_cash_allocation
      = MIN(
          global/config maximum cash per trade,
          strategy maximum cash cap,
          current available segment cash
        )

Therefore earlier losses can reduce the cash available for later trades.
Earlier profits/losses affect the CURRENT AVAILABLE segment balance according
to the final V2 capital-accounting rule.

Do not describe the original base allocation as permanently available for every
trade regardless of previous results.

4. Q-24 — DAILY TRADE LIMIT
---------------------------
Rama has clarified the key part:

MAXIMUM DAILY TRADE COUNT = FILLED TRADES.

Therefore:
- filled = consumes one daily trade slot
- queued/pending = does NOT consume a filled-trade slot
- cancelled = does NOT consume a filled-trade slot
- rejected = does NOT consume a filled-trade slot

However, this must NOT be interpreted as “pending orders consume no capital”.
Pending orders may still require capital reservation / broker capacity.
That question is separate from the FILLED-trade counter.

5. PENDING-ORDER / FILLED-LIMIT BEHAVIOUR
------------------------------------------
Configured maximum filled trades = 10.

If 9 trades have already FILLED:
- the system may submit the next eligible entry (10th slot).
- If that entry remains pending, the filled count is still 9.
- A later eligible entry may therefore be submitted, subject to all other
  capital, risk, duplicate, position, broker, and pending-order constraints.
- If that later order FILLS, the filled count becomes 10.
- Once the count reaches 10, block further new entries for that segment/day.

There is one important race condition:

If an earlier entry is still pending while a later entry fills, initiate
cancellation of the still-pending earlier entry.

If the earlier order FILLS before its cancellation is confirmed, accept the
broker’s actual fill. Do NOT pretend the fill did not happen.

In that bounded race case:
- the system may end up with 11 filled trades against a configured limit of 10;
- immediately block all further new entries;
- reconcile broker/local state;
- record the over-limit fill as a bounded execution race / exception;
- alert and preserve evidence;
- do not cancel/reverse a genuine fill merely to make the counter equal 10.

This is an execution-race exception, not normal permission to exceed the limit.

6. IMPORTANT WORDING
--------------------
Do not write:

“After 9 trades, submit unlimited additional orders until one fills.”

That would be unsafe and is NOT what Rama said.

Use:

submission remains subject to capital availability, pending-order controls,
strategy/risk limits, duplicate rules, position limits, broker constraints,
and any configured maximum outstanding/pending-order policy.”

If V2 has not yet defined a maximum number of simultaneous pending entry orders,
mark that control OPEN rather than inventing one.
