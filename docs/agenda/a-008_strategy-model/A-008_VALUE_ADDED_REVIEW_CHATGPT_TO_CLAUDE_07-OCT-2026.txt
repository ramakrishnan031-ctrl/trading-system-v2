1. IMPORTANT: DO NOT OVERSTATE THE "LATE SIGNAL" MECHANISM
The current wording that today's volume > 1.5x average volume and the other
daily conditions "can only become true after much of the day's move" or that
the volume condition is "only true late in the day" is too strong.

Use this classification:
FACT:
- scanners used the forming current-day daily values;
- conditions became true when those values crossed their thresholds;
- the system also had a global 10:00 entry floor and downstream processing
  delays.

INTERPRETATION / AUDIT LEAD:
- the conditions CAN produce late/extended signals;
- their combination may delay some signals;
- the volume threshold may be reached later depending on the stock's
  intraday volume curve.

UNKNOWN:
- every signal was late;
- the volume condition could only become true late;
- the exact delay attributable to the scanner;
- how much of poor performance came from scanner timing versus screening,
  price construction, entry timing or market regime.

Recommended wording:
"The scanners used the forming daily candle. Their conditions could become
true only after the current-day values crossed the specified thresholds and
therefore could produce signals after a substantial part of the move. This
is a credible mechanism for late signals, but the legacy evidence does not
establish that every signal was late or quantify the delay."
