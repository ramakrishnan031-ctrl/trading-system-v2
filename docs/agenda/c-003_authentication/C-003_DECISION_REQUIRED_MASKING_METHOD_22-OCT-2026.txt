2. Q-A (P-1): HOW MAY HOST RECORDS BE READ FROM NOW ON?
Option 1 - value-blind only (the lesson's own method).
  Host records are read only through narrow patterns whose results are counts or the presence of probe words (PASS or
  FAIL and line numbers). No host-record line is printed.
  - For C-003: enough. The independent final QA can test every record statement and every quoted phrase this way,
    and the manual needs no new record reading.
  - Cost: a paraphrase can be checked only through its probe words; record research in later topics is slower and
    depends on well-chosen probe words.
  - Risk: the lowest of the three (nothing from a host record is printed).
Option 2 - test an extended filter first; then printing through it, gated.
  Before any host-record line is printed again: extend the stage-3 mask and the residual counter for (a) host and
  domain names in quoted text, (b) SSH public-key material and key comments, (c) account and user names that are not
  e-mail addresses, and (d) other identifier-like values; test them on synthetic fixtures only (no real value),
  reporting class-level pass/fail counts; Rama approves the result; the method and the test result go into the design
  log. Printing then goes only through the tested filter and the gate.
  - For C-003: not needed, since option 1 covers the QA; it would delay the QA by the build and the test.
  - Cost: helper work in the scratch folder (research tooling, not V2 code) and a review of the test.
  - Risk: lower than now, not zero: a pattern scan does not prove that nothing else sensitive exists (lesson
    2026-10-10), and a class nobody listed can still pass.
Option 3 - keep the current stage-3 method as it is.
  Not recommended: E-1 showed three classes it misses.
Claude's recommendation, for Rama to decide: option 1 for the rest of C-003, the final QA included; option 2 only if
a later topic needs printed host-record text, decided at that point.
