0. FINDINGS IN BRIEF (details and citations in the sections below)
========================================================================================================================
- The stage: the processor calls screen() once per signal with market_data=None, the only caller in the authorised files
  (IN-01, IN-02). screen() runs step 0 (the MIS pre-drop), step 1 (one broker quote), the enforce branch or step 1b
  (D-002's), the ten executor steps, step 4b (step errors), the scorer, the shadow compare (D-002's), step 6 (minimum
  score), step 7 (signal age) and PASSED, and persists every outcome itself (SC-01 to SC-11, ST-01 to ST-04).
- Data: the only market input is the broker quote (last price, top bid and ask, volume, VWAP, OHLC, circuit limits);
  ATR, RSI, sector, previous close and 20-day average volume are always None, so volume_surge and atr_filter score 0 and
  rsi_range and sector_strength 0.5 on every signal. This answers Q-34 (c): the 65 ceiling (MD-01 to MD-07).
- Score: proportional to the weights present (FIX-042); pass 60 for all 16 strategies (min_score 0); tiers 80 and 65;
  under the ceiling a pass needed nearly every live step at full score and MEDIUM needed all of them; HIGH was
  unreachable (KNOWN DEFECT, recorded) (SQ-01 to SQ-09). The per-strategy thresholds did not tell strategies apart on
  that path (SQ-11). The tier reaches the sizer's multiplier and the retest diverter (SQ-10).
- Execution: all ten steps always run, each on one shared single-worker pool with a 5 s timeout (the default; main.py
  passes none); a timeout scores 0.5 and replaces the pool; an exception makes the result REJECTED_STEP_ERROR (SE-01 to
  SE-05, SC-07).
- MIS: step 0 reads the learned blocklist and, on the repository values (enabled, shadow, 1-day TTL), logs and never
  rejects; the writer is the order placer's 400 handler, G-007's (MS-01 to MS-06).
- No S&R, regime or V3-chain input reaches the screener; its only V3 element is the hard-gate re-scale (shadow); S&R
  zones are read after it, by the retest diverter (SR-01 to SR-04).
- Hand-off: PASSED is internal; the processor goes on to price derivation, the retest divert, sizing with the tier, the
  throttle and placement (HO-01 to HO-06).
- For review, labelled INTERPRETATION: max_spread_pct and min_adr_pct are 0.005 in every YAML against percent
  arithmetic, while the tests use 0.1 and 0.5 (MD-09); a quote without OHLC would make price_action raise and the result
  REJECTED_STEP_ERROR (MD-08); on the processor's path a signal_age or circuit_check of 0.0 ends REJECTED_SCORE_<n>
  before step 7 (SQ-08).
