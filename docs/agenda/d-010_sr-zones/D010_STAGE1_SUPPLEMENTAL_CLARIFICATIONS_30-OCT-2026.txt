3. CLARIFICATION 2 (RC-02): THE "NEXT EXPECTED PRICE RANGE" AFTER A BREAKOUT OR BREAKDOWN
========================================================================================================================
Line 34:
  Q: Rama-authorised clarification, drafted by ChatGPT:
Lines 36, 38, 40 and 42:
  Q: "The next expected price range is not the same thing as the S&R area that price has just broken. It refers to the next historically identified S&R reference or reaction area in the direction of the price movement.
  Q: For example, if the stock is approaching a resistance area and breaks above it, the trader looks further back in the historical chart to identify the next relevant resistance area above the breakout. Similarly, if the stock breaks below a support area, the trader looks for the next relevant support area below it.
  Q: So, the S&R area being tested or broken is one reference; the next historical S&R area in the direction of movement is a separate reference. It is a possible area for price to reach or react around, not a guaranteed destination or prediction.
  Q: The BANKBARODA example illustrates this distinction: the area around INR277--INR280 is the resistance area being approached/tested in the example, while the lower historical area around INR256--INR257 is a separate support reference. The example does not establish a general calculation rule or guarantee that price will travel from one area to the other."
ChatGPT's limits (lines 45 to 48):
  Q: - "Next" means the next relevant historical S&R reference/area in the direction being considered, not the same broken/tested area.
  Q: - This is a clarification of terminology and intent only.
  Q: - It does not define how to rank competing areas, calculate "nearest", select a timeframe, estimate probability, set a target, calculate R:R, or decide Trade/Reject.
  Q: - D-009 OQ9-13 and OQ9-14 remain OPEN. Target and R:R mechanics remain with their proper E topics.
