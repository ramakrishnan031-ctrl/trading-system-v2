The first-pass source review by ChatGPT found these starting points to verify, not blindly accept:
- README.md says main.py self-exits cleanly after the service window.
- config/system_config.yaml declares trading_hours.service_window_end: "17:35", with comments explaining the historical change from a hardcoded 16:00.
- main.py has an EOD window-end self-exit watchdog; it is armed only for normal starts and skipped for --interactive, --resume, or TS_IGNORE_MARKET_WINDOW=1.
- The watchdog is intended to wait until the configured window end and until positions requiring this service are absent; it also gates on HARD_KILL flatten-in-progress.
- main.py then waits on _shutdown_event and calls _shutdown(...); exact signal registration, shutdown ordering, each component's stop/close behaviour, and error handling require a complete source trace.
- deploy/systemd/trading-system.service declares KillSignal=SIGINT, TimeoutStopSec=30, Restart=on-failure and RestartPreventExitStatus=3 4 5. This is a repository declaration only, not proof of the installed unit.
- The source comments describe a 15-second bounded drain for a HARD_KILL flatten on external stop, because the service stop timeout is 30 seconds; the internal EOD self-exit waits on the flatten gate. Verify the executable path and limits.
These are search leads, not a substitute for answering every question below.
