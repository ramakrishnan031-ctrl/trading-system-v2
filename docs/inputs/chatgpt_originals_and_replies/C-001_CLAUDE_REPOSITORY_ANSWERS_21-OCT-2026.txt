2.3 Relationship of scanner keys, strategy names, directions and webhook identifiers (Q2b)
  F-05 FACT (SOURCE-VERIFIED). In all 16 entries the map's strategy value equals the scanner key, and the 16
       strategy values are distinct. The schema allows several scanners to name one strategy (N:1): the map is a
       dict of ScannerEntry with no uniqueness rule on strategy values.
       Evidence: config/scan_webhook_map.yaml lines 17 to 84; core/config_loader.py lines 2347 to 2377.
  F-06 FACT (SOURCE-VERIFIED). The webhook identifier is the URL path segment: the route is
       POST /webhook/<scanner_name>, and the segment must be a key of AppConfig.scan_webhook_map.scanners or the
       request gets HTTP 404. No other webhook identifier exists in the repository.
       Evidence: signals/webhook_receiver.py lines 406 to 408 (route); signals/webhook_receiver.py lines 551 to 554 (404).
       Comment claim: config/scan_webhook_map.yaml lines 77 to 80 say the routing key must equal the path "Rama pastes into Chartink".
       Caveat: the webhook URLs actually configured on Chartink are UNKNOWN (U-01).
  F-07 FACT (SOURCE-VERIFIED). The webhook carries no side. The receiver reads only stocks, trigger_prices,
       triggered_at and the optional scan_name; direction is taken at processing time from the mapped strategy
       object. Matches DR-023 ("the webhook carries no side", SUPPLIED RECORD (INDIRECT)).
       Evidence: signals/webhook_receiver.py lines 608 to 674 (fields read).
       Evidence: signals/signal_processor.py lines 933 to 953 (strategy lookup); signals/signal_processor.py lines 997 to 1004 (direction=strategy_obj.direction).
