SUMMARY
==============================================================================
- Three repeat guards run in order for every intraday symbol, all inside R _process_signal (R 892-1072), after the validity and
  expiry checks: (1) the in-memory in-flight claim, keyed by symbol only -> IN_PROCESS; (2) the in-memory TTL cache, keyed by
  (symbol, scanner) for dedup_window_seconds (300 in the repository config) -> DUPLICATE; (3) the database unique index on
  (fingerprint, fingerprint_date), the fingerprint being SHA-256 of "scanner|symbol|epoch bucket" -> DUPLICATE, or the re-queue
  of an earlier QUEUE_FULL row. FACT (SOURCE-VERIFIED).
- The claim is released by the receiver on every refusal after it (R 944, 1000, 1014, 1031, 1067; not on the C-004 L-13
  exception path) and by the processor when it finishes (P 1248-1271), except while a signal is re-queued or handed to the
  allocator. A sweeper evicts any claim with no heartbeat for more than 60 s; no runtime code renews the heartbeat at the pin
  (B-007's known defect, re-verified).
- The frozen statements on the claim's lifetime (B-009, C-004 L-13, B-007, C-002) agree once placed in one table (section 4);
  two further latent points are recorded as leads, not resolved (C5-L1, C5-L2).
- No paper/live branch exists in R. The EOD route and the replay tool do not use any of the three guards.
