3. CONFLICTS TO RESOLVE BEFORE A REQUIREMENTS RECORD
========================================================================================================================
- C-1: RQ8-04 (record market context) requires a definition of what is recorded. The D-008 boundary (Stage 0
  prompt) excludes deciding "the trend formula, indicators, lookbacks, timeframe hierarchy". A requirements record
  can state that context is recorded without saying what context is.
  - Proposal: D-008 records the requirement and leaves the definition OPEN for a named later step, rather than
    widening D-008's boundary.
- C-2: the pre-flight check in Q4 must not act as a gate (RQ8-01).
  - Proposal: the requirements record states it as a warning-class check that alerts on both channels and blocks
    nothing.
