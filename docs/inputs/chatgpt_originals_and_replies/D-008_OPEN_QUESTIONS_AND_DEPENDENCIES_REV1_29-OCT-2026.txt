3. CONFLICTS TO RESOLVE BEFORE A REQUIREMENTS RECORD
========================================================================================================================
- C-1: RQ8-04 (record market context) requires a definition of what is recorded. The D-008 boundary (Stage 0
  prompt) excludes deciding "the trend formula, indicators, lookbacks, timeframe hierarchy".
  - Proposal: D-008 records the high-level requirement only. It is not implementation-ready until the later step
    named under OQ8-13 defines the minimum record fields, timestamps, source and provenance, quality state and
    storage. D-008's boundary is not widened.
- C-2: the market-context pre-flight check in Q4 must not act as a gate (RQ8-01).
  - Proposal: the requirements record states that this check is warning / alert-only. It alerts on both channels
    and cannot block a trade or the system start solely because market-context data is missing, late or suspect.
  - The proposal covers this check only. Other pre-flight checks, existing or future, keep their own authority.
