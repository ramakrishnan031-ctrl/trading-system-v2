Your answer: Support & Resistance (S&R) means that In Stock trading where the current stock's price (CMP) is going to settled (meaning either upward or downward) once certain price or point is break-out or break-down, Why? a professional trader always looks at chart and takes reference from historical movement when certain price is break-out or Break-down then what's the next possible price to be reached from current price (refer the attached BANKBARODA chart where Blue line is resistance and Green line is Support identification purpose)
1. Resistance (Blue line): Assume we are in 19-Apr-2026 where CMP is Rs 272 then trade sees this chart then finds out where is 'Next' resistance zone in 1D chart? as per historical movement it is Rs 277.95 which is the multiple touches whenever this price is reached then stock is either raising (break-out) or falling (break-down) refer the probable dates: 26-Nov-2025 to till 2026, again Feb-Mar periods, So assumes let's short at Rs 277.95 ( meaning Current date is 19-Apr-2026 with CMP 272 and waits to reach price is Rs 277.95) then shorts at Rs 277.95 on 20-Apr-2026 (assumed stock reaches it's resistance based on historical chart/point), then checks where it's 'Support zone' as per historical chart it is around Rs 256.86 (March 12- April), So now trader shorts at Rs 277.95 then puts TGT at Rs 256.86 then SL is probably 284 (drawn is chart as 'SL') which is also resistance at Feb 10 - 19 period, then same way stock falls as on 20-Apr-2026 from Rs 277.95 to 256.56

2. Support (Green Zone): same way assume that we are in June 1- 10 period where CMP is Rs 256.86, then trader saw this chart to find where is next S&R once is price is break-out then it is Rs 277.95 (20-Apr-2025 price) then makes long order with entry at Rs 256.86, TGT 277.95 then SL around Rs 240 (which is price in between 'April' market as '-->' in chart) the same way stock made it is journey

** So basically, S&R means it is expected price points when certain price is broken (rais/falls) then where is the next expected price range stock is going to settled (What is the resistance or support) by reviewing the historical chart with same CMP

Your answer: Since professional traders (real market movers of market) always reviews the historical chart to see where is next expected price is to be settled or reached? instead of assuming himself this is the price randomly (Example: CMP is Rs 100 then add random number like 1 or 2 or 3 or 1% or 2% or something else randomly), Basically all traders checks the historical charts and tries to move the stock's price (either upward/downward) till the expected price is reached, Once price is reached then stock's makes consolidation (ie, Basically traders will books current profit/SL then waits for next movement should be upward/downward again)

Shortly, All traders tries to move  or continue the stock movement when certain price is broken (break-out or down) to some price which is based on historical price touch, once that expected price is touched then there will not such further immediate movement of same stock (ie, consolidation phase of stock), So we must follow the same logic instead of playing with random numbers/prices
